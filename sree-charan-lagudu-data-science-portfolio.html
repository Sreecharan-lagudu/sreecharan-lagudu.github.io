About me

Master's student in Data Science & Analytics at EPITA, Paris — building real systems, not just notebooks.

I'm a data science graduate student who believes a model isn't finished when the metrics look good — it's finished when someone can use it, question it, and trust it.

My projects span the full lifecycle: ingestion with Airflow, validation with Great Expectations, storage in PostgreSQL, serving through FastAPI, dashboards in Streamlit, and monitoring with Grafana. I care about explainability (SHAP), honest metrics (AUC over accuracy on imbalanced data), and security (credentials never in code).

When something breaks, I dig in — like the day a live demo's dataset turned out to contain month names in a numeric column, and I traced it to a pandas 3 dtype change at 1 a.m.

Water Quality Prediction — MLOps Pipeline

A production-style pipeline: Airflow ingests raw CSV chunks on a schedule, Great Expectations validates every record, and PostgreSQL routes clean vs. suspect data into good_data_table / bad_data_table. A RandomForest model (22 sensor features) is served via FastAPI and visualized in Streamlit, with Grafana dashboards for monitoring.
