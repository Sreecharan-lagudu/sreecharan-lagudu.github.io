About me

Master's student in Data Science & Analytics at EPITA, Paris — building real systems, not just notebooks.

Before my Master's, I spent 2+ years as an Assistant System Analyst at C-EDGE Technologies (a TCS–SBI banking-tech joint venture in Hyderabad) — where I learned that stakeholders need decisions, not just dashboards. Now I'm a graduate student who believes a model isn't finished when the metrics look good — it's finished when someone can use it, question it, and trust it.

My projects span the full lifecycle: ingestion with Airflow, validation with Great Expectations, storage in PostgreSQL, serving through FastAPI, dashboards in Streamlit, and monitoring with Grafana. I care about explainability (SHAP), honest metrics (AUC over accuracy on imbalanced data), and security (credentials never in code).

Water Quality Prediction — MLOps Pipeline

A production-style pipeline: Airflow ingests raw CSV chunks on a schedule, Great Expectations validates every record, and PostgreSQL routes clean vs. suspect data into good_data_table / bad_data_table.
